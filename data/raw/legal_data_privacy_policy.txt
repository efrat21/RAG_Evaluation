# ACME GLOBAL TECHNOLOGIES - DATA PRIVACY AND GOVERNANCE POLICY
Policy Document: POL-LEG-2025-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Supervisory Authority: Global Legal & Compliance Department
Data Protection Officer (DPO): privacy@acmeglobal.com / dpo@acmeglobal.com
Jurisdictions Covered: Global (GDPR - EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA - California, APPI - Japan, PDPA - Singapore)

## 1. Statement of Intent and Scope
Acme Global Technologies ("Acme", "we", "our") is committed to upholding the highest standards of data protection, fundamental privacy rights, and regulatory compliance. As an enterprise software provider operating cloud infrastructure across North America, Europe, the United Kingdom, and the Asia-Pacific region, we routinely process personal data belonging to employees, enterprise customers, prospective leads, and website visitors. This Data Privacy and Governance Policy establishes binding operational guidelines for the lawful collection, processing, storage, transfer, and deletion of personal data.

## 2. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliance
Acme adheres to the core data processing principles set forth under Article 5 of the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR) and the UK GDPR:
- Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency: Personal data must only be processed where a valid legal basis exists (e.g., performance of contract, compliance with legal obligations, explicit user consent, or legitimate business interest). Clear privacy notices must be provided at the point of data capture.
- Purpose Limitation: Data collected for one specified business objective must not be repurposed without additional consent or lawful basis.
- Data Minimization: Squads and data pipelines must only collect the minimum volume of personal attributes strictly required to perform the intended service function.
- Accuracy: Automated and manual data management mechanisms must be established to ensure records are kept up to date and inaccurate data is rectified without delay.
- Storage Limitation: Data must not be kept in identifiable form longer than necessary for the underlying business purpose.
- Integrity and Confidentiality: Technical and organizational security measures—including AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, and pseudonymization—must be enforced.

## 3. Data Subject Rights (DSR) Management
Under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy frameworks, individuals are endowed with enforceable statutory privacy rights:
1. Right of Access: Individuals have the right to request a complete copy of their personal data held in Acme systems.
2. Right to Rectification: Right to correct inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete data.
3. Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Right to demand permanent deletion of personal records, subject to statutory retention exceptions.
4. Right to Restriction of Processing: Right to suspend active processing during dispute resolution.
5. Right to Data Portability: Right to receive data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format (JSON or CSV).
6. Right to Object / Opt-Out: Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing profiling.

Handling DSR Inquiries: All Data Subject Requests received via email, web form (https://privacy.acmeglobal.com/dsr), or customer support must be forwarded immediately to privacy@acmeglobal.com. Under statutory mandates, Acme must fulfill verified DSR requests within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt.

## 4. Statutory Data Retention Schedules
To comply with global corporate, tax, labor, and privacy statutes, Acme enforces rigid data retention periods across all data repositories and cloud data lakes:
- Financial and Accounting Records: Seven (7) years. Includes audited general ledgers, tax filings, payroll disbursements, vendor invoices, customer billing records, and expense submissions. Required under US IRS guidelines and international commercial codes.
- Human Resources and Employee Records: Five (5) years following the official date of employee separation. Includes employment contracts, performance evaluations, disciplinary records, benefit elections, and workers' compensation filings.
- General Business Communications and Operational Records: Three (3) years. Includes general customer support tickets, sales prospect communications, routine vendor correspondence, internal Slack message archives, and system operational logs.
- Automated Data Purging: Following the expiration of the applicable retention schedule, data stored in production databases, S3 storage buckets, and archival snapshots must be systematically and irreversibly purged or permanently anonymized.

## 5. Security Data Breach Notification Procedure (72-Hour Mandate)
A personal data breach entails any confirmed or suspected security incident resulting in the accidental, unlawful, or unauthorized destruction, loss, alteration, disclosure of, or access to personal data:
- Immediate Internal Escalation: Any team member detecting evidence of compromised customer data, unauthorized database dumps, or lost unencrypted media must notify the Incident Response Team and Legal Department within one (1) hour of discovery via emergency hotline or `#security-incidents` on Slack.
- 72-Hour Regulatory Notification Clock: Under Article 33 of the GDPR, Acme is legally obligated to notify the competent Lead Data Protection Authority (such as the Irish Data Protection Commission or UK Information Commissioner's Office) without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of a high-risk personal data breach.
- Data Subject Notifications: If the breach presents a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, Acme must notify affected data subjects without unreasonable delay, providing clear remedial guidance and mitigation measures.

## 6. Data Processing Addenda (DPA) and Vendor Governance
Acme contracts with third-party sub-processors (such as AWS, Datadog, Salesforce, and Okta) to support our cloud infrastructure:
- Mandatory DPA Execution: Prior to sharing or transmitting any customer or employee personal data with an external vendor or SaaS service provider, a legally binding Data Processing Addendum (DPA) incorporating Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) must be fully executed.
- Sub-Processor Auditing: The Legal Department maintains a public list of authorized sub-processors at https://acmeglobal.com/legal/subprocessors. Customers must be given at least thirty (30) days' advance notice before onboarding any new sub-processor.

## 7. International Cross-Border Data Transfers
Transfers of personal data originating in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland to jurisdictions without an adequacy decision (such as the United States) must be governed by valid transfer mechanisms:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): Acme incorporates the European Commission's approved 2021 Standard Contractual Clauses across all customer and vendor master agreements.
- Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs): Legal and InfoSec must perform formal TIAs evaluating surveillance legislation in recipient countries and verifying supplementary technical safeguards (end-to-end encryption, customer-managed KMS keys).

For questions regarding privacy assessments, data classification, or regulatory inquiries, contact the Acme Legal Privacy Office at privacy@acmeglobal.com.
