# ACME GLOBAL TECHNOLOGIES - COMPREHENSIVE EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE
Plan Year: 2025
Administered by: Acme Total Rewards & People Operations Team
Contact: benefits@acmeglobal.com | Benefits Hotline: +1 (800) 555-0199

## 1. Overview of Benefits Package
At Acme Global Technologies, we take pride in supporting the health, financial well-being, and family balance of our global workforce. Our comprehensive total rewards package is designed to provide best-in-class coverage starting on your very first day of regular, full-time employment. Annual Open Enrollment occurs every November, during which you can modify your elections for the upcoming calendar year. Qualifying life events (such as marriage, birth, or legal adoption) allow for enrollment modifications within 30 calendar days of the event.

## 2. Health and Medical Insurance Coverage
Our primary health insurance offering in the United States is the Blue Cross Blue Shield Preferred Provider Organization (Blue Cross PPO Plan).
- Plan Design: Offers both in-network and out-of-network flexibility without requiring primary care physician (PCP) referrals for specialist consultations.
- Employee Premium Contribution: The standard individual employee premium contribution is $200.00 per month, deducted semi-monthly in equal pre-tax installments of $100.00. Family and spousal tier coverage options are also available with adjusted pre-tax payroll deductions.
- Deductibles and Out-of-Pocket Maximums: The annual individual in-network deductible is $750.00, and the family in-network deductible is $1,500.00. The annual out-of-pocket maximum is capped at $3,500.00 per individual and $7,000.00 per family.
- Preventive Care: In-network annual preventive physicals, well-woman examinations, and routine pediatric immunizations are covered at 100% with no copayment or coinsurance.
- Prescription Drug Tiers: Generic prescriptions carry a $10.00 copay; preferred brand-name prescriptions carry a $30.00 copay; non-preferred and specialty medications require a 20% coinsurance payment up to a $150.00 monthly cap.
- Telehealth Services: 24/7 on-demand virtual medical consultations are available via Teladoc with a $0 copayment for enrolled members.

## 3. Dental and Vision Coverage
- Dental Plan (Delta Dental Premier): Comprehensive preventive, basic, and major restorative services. Preventive checkups and cleanings (twice per year) are covered at 100%. Basic procedures (fillings, root canals) are covered at 80% after a $50 annual individual deductible. Major restorative procedures (crowns, bridges, implants) are covered at 50% up to an annual maximum benefit of $2,500 per covered person. Orthodontia is covered at 50% with a lifetime maximum of $2,000 per child.
- Vision Plan (VSP Vision Care): Annual eye examinations are covered with a $10 copayment. Prescription lens copay is $20 for standard single vision or progressive lenses. Frame allowance is $200 annually, with a 20% discount applied to amounts exceeding the allowance. An alternative $180 annual contact lens allowance is provided in lieu of eyeglasses frames.

## 4. Retirement Savings: 401(k) Plan and Employer Match
Acme partners with Fidelity Investments to offer both traditional pre-tax 401(k) and Roth post-tax 401(k) investment vehicles.
- Company Match: Acme provides a dollar-for-dollar matching contribution of 100% on the first 4% of eligible base salary contributed by the employee. For example, if you contribute 4% of your $120,000 annual salary ($4,800), Acme contributes an additional matching $4,800.
- Vesting Schedule: All employer matching contributions feature immediate 100% vesting from day one. There is no vesting cliff or waiting period.
- Contribution Limits: Employees may contribute up to the annual IRS statutory maximum limit ($23,500 for 2025, with an additional $7,500 catch-up contribution for employees aged 50 and older). Auto-enrollment defaults to 4% pre-tax upon 30 days of employment unless an alternate election is configured via NetBenefits.fidelity.com.

## 5. Life and Disability Insurance
- Basic Life and AD&D Insurance: Fully paid by Acme at no cost to the employee. Provides group term life coverage equivalent to 2x your annual base salary, up to a maximum policy benefit of $500,000.
- Supplemental Voluntary Life Insurance: Employees may elect additional life coverage up to 5x salary or $1,000,000, subject to medical evidence of insurability.
- Short-Term Disability (STD): Replaces 66.6% of base salary up to $2,500 per week for up to 13 weeks following an elimination period of 7 calendar days. Fully funded by Acme.
- Long-Term Disability (LTD): Takes effect on the 91st day of continuous disability, replacing 60% of base salary up to $10,000 per month until retirement age. Fully funded by Acme.

## 6. Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)
Acme offers tax-advantaged Flexible Spending Accounts managed through Wex Health:
- Healthcare FSA: Contribute up to $3,200 annually pre-tax to pay for qualifying medical, dental, and vision out-of-pocket expenses. Up to $640 of unused funds may roll over into the following plan year.
- Dependent Care FSA: Contribute up to $5,000 annually pre-tax for eligible childcare (children under age 13) or eldercare expenses. Use-it-or-lose-it rule applies at plan year end.

## 7. Parental Leave Policy
Acme recognizes the profound importance of supporting growing families:
- Primary Caregiver Leave: 16 weeks of 100% fully paid leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- Secondary Caregiver Leave: 8 weeks of 100% fully paid leave.
- Eligibility: All full-time regular employees who have completed at least six continuous months of service are eligible. Leave may be taken continuously or in two separate blocks within the first 12 months following the qualifying event. Acme continues all standard health and welfare benefits during paid parental leave.

## 8. Wellness and Fitness Reimbursement
To champion physical and mental wellness, Acme reimburses up to $50.00 per month ($600 per year) toward qualifying fitness and wellness memberships. Qualifying expenses include traditional gym memberships, boutique fitness studios (Pilates, yoga, CrossFit), virtual fitness subscriptions (Peloton, Apple Fitness+), and municipal recreation passes. Submit monthly receipts through the Expensify portal under the "Wellness Reimbursement" expense category with payment proof.
